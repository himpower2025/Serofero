Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026 · Himpower Pvt. Ltd.

1. Who we are

Serofero is a local second-hand marketplace operated by Himpower Pvt. Ltd., a company registered in Nepal. This policy explains what personal data the Serofero mobile apps and website collect, why we collect it, who processes it on our behalf, and how you can have it deleted. It applies to the iOS app, the Android app and the web app.

Contact for any privacy question or request: himpower2025@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google or with Apple, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from that provider, plus a unique account identifier. We never receive or store your password. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only ever see Apple’s private relay address.
  • Listings you publish. Item title, description, category, price, the neighbourhood you type in, and the photos you upload. Listings are public to all signed-in Serofero users.
  • Messages. The content and timestamps of chat messages you exchange with other users about a listing.
  • Photos and camera. Only the images you explicitly choose or capture for a listing are uploaded. We do not access your photo library in the background.
  • Location. If you grant permission, we read your device’s approximate location while the app is open, to show listings near you. We do not track your location in the background and we do not store your coordinates on our servers.
  • Reports and support tickets. The content of any abuse report or support request you send, together with your account identifier and contact email, so we can investigate and reply.
  • Local device settings. Your saved-items list, avatar choices and blocked-user list are stored on your own device.

We do not collect payment card details. Serofero does not process payments — all payment happens directly between users, offline.

3. How we use it

  • To create and authenticate your account.
  • To publish your listings and let other neighbours contact you about them.
  • To deliver chat messages and show alerts about items you saved.
  • To review reports of objectionable content and to enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To diagnose crashes and technical faults.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Service providers

We use a small number of processors, and your data is stored on their infrastructure rather than our own servers:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) — authentication, database, file storage and hosting for listings, chats, reports and account records.
  • Google Gemini API (Google LLC) — if you tap “generate description”, the item name and category you entered are sent to Google’s Gemini model to draft suggested text. No account identifier and no chat content is sent.
  • Vercel Inc. — hosting for the Serofero web app.

These providers may store and process data outside Nepal, including in the United States and the European Union.

5. What other users can see

Your display name, profile photo, trust score and the listings you publish are visible to every signed-in Serofero user. The neighbourhood you enter on a listing is visible on that listing. Your email address, your precise location and your chat history with other people are not public.

6. Retention

Listings, chats and account records are kept until you delete them or delete your account. Abuse reports are kept for up to 24 months after they are resolved, even if the reporter deletes their account, because they are safety records concerning another user’s conduct; they are retained without the reporter’s contact details where possible.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete any individual listing from the Profile tab at any time.

You can permanently delete your entire account from Profile → Compliance Center → Delete Account, inside the app. This erases your account, every listing you published, your chat threads, your support tickets and all settings stored on your device. The action cannot be undone. For security you may be asked to sign in again first.

If you cannot access the app, email himpower2025@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you within 30 days.

8. Children

Serofero is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored on Firebase infrastructure with access restricted by server-side security rules. No online service can be guaranteed completely secure, so please do not share sensitive personal documents through chat.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data we will update this page and revise the date at the top. Continued use of Serofero after a change means you accept the updated policy.
